Before you order

  • Shipping is an estimate quoted to Richmond, VA. Your ZIP will change it. Totals leave out sales tax.
  • Silver Seal discs come in packs of 4. Piston Depot accepts orders for them but lists 0 on hand, so ask about lead time.
  • BP shims are nominally 27 mm (26.95 mm actual). Toyota fit comes from the part list this tool is based on and has not been separately confirmed, so measure one before ordering a full set.
